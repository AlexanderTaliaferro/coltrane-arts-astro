# Image Assets

Place the following image files in this folder. Filenames must match exactly — they are referenced from the components.

## Hero
- `coltrane-portrait.jpg` — The Emory Douglas charcoal portrait (used as hero background)
- `og-image.jpg` — Social share card, 1200×630 recommended (optional)

## Events
- `diRosaEvent.png` — Small thumbnail for the A Love Supreme fundraiser tile

## Gallery slideshow
These four images are cycled as the user scrolls the provenance timeline:
- `black-panther-1972.jpg` — 1972 newspaper clipping (step 1)
- `coltrane-1972.jpg` — Full 'Coltrane' 1972/2024 painting (step 2)
- `double-signature.jpg` — Close-up of Emory's double signature (step 3)
- `spike-lee-2024.jpg` — Spike Lee at Shaw's studio, July 2024 (step 4)

## Board
- `board-franzo.jpg` — Archbishop Franzo King portrait
- `board-marina.jpg` — Reverend Mother Marina King portrait
- `board-shaw.jpg` — Peter Shaw portrait

## Notes
- Format: JPG (or PNG where noted), 80% quality is fine
- Recommended sizes: portrait images ~1200×1600, hero ~2400×1600
- The site applies a sepia archival treatment in CSS, so source images can be either color or black & white.
